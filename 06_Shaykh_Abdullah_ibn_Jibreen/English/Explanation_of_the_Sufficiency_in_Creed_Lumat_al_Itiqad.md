# Explanation of the Sufficiency in Creed (Lum'at al-I'tiqad)
### Author: Shaykh Abdullah ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Jibreen (1352–1430 AH)
### English Translation of Selected Core Lessons

---

## Introduction by the Commentator

All praise is due to Allah alone, and peace and blessings be upon the Prophet Muhammad, his family, and Companions.

The matn *Lum'at al-I'tiqad al-Hadi ila Sabil al-Rashad* by Imam Muwaffaq al-Din Ibn Qudamah al-Maqdisi (d. 620 AH) represents one of the most celebrated and concise classical expositions of the creed of Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah according to the methodology of the Salaf al-Salih.

In this commentary, we elucidate the key foundations that protect the Muslim from the scholastic deviations of the Jahmiyyah, Mu'tazilah, and Ash'ariyyah.

---

## Foundation 1: Affirming Allah's Divine Names and Attributes

The foundational methodology of the Companions, Successors, and the four Imams regarding the Names and Attributes of Allah encompasses three critical principles:

1. **Affirmation without Anthropomorphism (*Ithbat bila Tashbih*)**:
   Whatever Allah has affirmed for Himself in the Qur'an or through the tongue of His noble Messenger ﷺ must be affirmed in a manner befitting His majesty.
   When Allah states:
   > *"The Most Gracious established Himself above the Throne (*al-Rahmanu 'ala al-'Arshi istawa*)"* (Surah Ta-Ha 20:5)
   We affirm the reality of the Istiwa' (Ascension and Exaltation above the Throne) while rejecting any resemblance to creation. As Imam Malik famously replied: *"The Istiwa' is known, the modality (*kayf*) is incomprehensible, believing in it is obligatory, and questioning concerning its modality is an innovation."*

2. **Negation of Deficiency without Negation of Meaning (*Nafi bila Ta'til*)**:
   We negate from Allah whatever He has negated from Himself—such as slumber, ignorance, death, injustice, and physical limitations—without stripping the texts of their authentic theological realities.

3. **Rejection of Figurative Distortion (*Tahreef / Ta'weel*)**:
   Distorting the Hand of Allah (*Yadullah*) to mean mere "power" (*qudrah*) or "blessing" (*ni'mah*) is an innovation borrowed from non-Salafi theological schools. Ahl al-Sunnah affirm that Allah possesses two Hands befitting His majesty and glory.

---

## Foundation 2: Destiny and Divine Decree (*Al-Qadar*)

Belief in Qadar is the sixth pillar of Iman. A servant's faith is not sound until he firmly believes in its four universal levels (*Maratib al-Qadar*):

1. **Eternal Knowledge (*Al-Ilm*)**: Allah knows all things eternally, past, present, future, and that which will never occur—how it would be if it were to occur.
2. **The Inscription (*Al-Kitabah*)**: Allah recorded all destinies in the Preserved Tablet (*al-Lawh al-Mahfuz*) fifty thousand years before the creation of the heavens and earth.
3. **The Divine Will (*Al-Mashi'ah*)**: Whatever Allah wills occurs, and whatever He does not will never comes to pass.
4. **The Creation (*Al-Khalq*)**: Allah is the Creator of all entities, actions, and occurrences.

Yet, humans possess authentic free will and choice (*Ikhtiyar*) and bear complete moral accountability for their righteous and sinful deeds.
