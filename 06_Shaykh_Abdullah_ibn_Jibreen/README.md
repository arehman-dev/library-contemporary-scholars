# Allamah Faqih Shaykh Abdullah ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Jibreen
### العلامة الفقيه الشيخ عبد الله بن عبد الرحمن بن جبرين
**Lifespan:** 1352–1430 AH (1933–2009 CE)  
**Scholarly Role:** Senior Member of the Permanent Committee for Scholarly Research and Ifta  

---

## 📖 Biography & Scholarly Standing

Shaykh Abdullah ibn Jibreen was an encyclopedic scholar of Hanbali jurisprudence, Hadith, and theological polemics. Born in al-Quway'iyyah, he memorized the Qur'an and core mutun at an early age before studying under Shaykh Muhammad ibn Ibrahim and Shaykh Abd al-Aziz ibn Baz. He earned his master's and doctorate degrees from the Higher Institute of Judiciary and taught thousands of students through daily public halaqat at Riyadh mosques.
