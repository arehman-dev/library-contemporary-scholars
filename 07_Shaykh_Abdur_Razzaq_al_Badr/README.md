# Shaykh Dr. Abdur-Razzaq ibn Abdil-Muhsin al-Badr
### فضيلة الشيخ الدكتور عبد الرزاق بن عبد المحسن البدر
**Lifespan:** b. 1382 AH (1962 CE – Present)  
**Scholarly Role:** Professor of Aqidah at Islamic University of Madinah, Teacher at the Prophet's Mosque  

---

## 📖 Biography & Scholarly Standing

Shaykh Abdur-Razzaq al-Badr is a world-renowned contemporary educator, author, and specialist in Islamic creed and spiritual rectification (*Tazkiyah*). The son of the venerable traditionist Shaykh Abdul-Muhsin al-Abbad, he completed his doctorate in Islamic Creed at the Islamic University of Madinah. He is known globally for his accessible commentaries on core classical theological poems, treatises on supplicatory remembrance (*Dhikr*), and annual international teaching tours.
