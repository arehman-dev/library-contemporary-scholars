# Muhaddith of the Era Shaykh Muhammad Nasir al-Din al-Albani
### محدث العصر الشيخ محمد ناصر الدين الألبانی
**Lifespan:** 1333–1420 AH (1914–1999 CE)  
**Scholarly Role:** Foremost Hadith Scholar, Professor of Hadith Sciences at Islamic University of Madinah  

---

## 📖 Biography & Scholarly Standing

Shaykh Muhammad Nasir al-Din al-Albani was universally recognized as the reviving traditionist (*Muhaddith*) of the 14th century AH. Born in Shkodër, Albania, his family migrated to Damascus, Syria, where he mastered watchmaking while immersing himself in the rare manuscript treasures of the Zahiriyyah National Library. He dedicated over sixty years to the rigorous critical verification (*Tahqiq* and *Takhrij*) of prophetic traditions, revolutionizing modern Hadith scholarship through works such as Silsilat al-Ahadith al-Sahihah and Silsilat al-Ahadith al-Da'ifah.
