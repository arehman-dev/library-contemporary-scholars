# Shaykh Muhammad ibn Salih al-Uthaymeen
### العلامة الفقيه الشيخ محمد بن صالح العثيمين
**Lifespan:** 1347–1421 AH (1929–2001 CE)  
**Scholarly Role:** Senior Faqih, Professor at Imam Muhammad ibn Saud Islamic University, Member of Council of Senior Scholars  

---

## 📖 Biography & Scholarly Standing

Shaykh Muhammad ibn Salih al-Uthaymeen was the premier jurist (*Faqih*) of his era, distinguished by extraordinary pedagogical clarity, analytical rigor, and mastery of Hanbali jurisprudence and Usul al-Fiqh. Born in Unayzah, Qassim, he studied deeply under Shaykh Abd al-Rahman al-Sa'di and later Shaykh Abd al-Aziz ibn Baz. He taught daily for over four decades at the Grand Mosque of Unayzah and the Prophet's Mosque in Madinah, authoring definitive commentaries on Aqidah, Fiqh, Hadith, and Arabic grammar.
