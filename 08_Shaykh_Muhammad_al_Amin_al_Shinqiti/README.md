# Allamah Usuli Shaykh Muhammad al-Amin al-Shinqiti
### العلامة الأصولي الشيخ محمد الأمين الشنقيطي
**Lifespan:** 1325–1393 AH (1905–1973 CE)  
**Scholarly Role:** Master of Legal Theory and Exegesis, Professor at the Islamic University of Madinah  

---

## 📖 Biography & Scholarly Standing

Shaykh Muhammad al-Amin al-Shinqiti was an unparalleled authority in legal theory (*Usul al-Fiqh*), Qur'anic hermeneutics, and classical Arabic linguistics. Born in Mauritania (Shinqit), he memorized the Qur'an with all variant readings, the Maliki legal manuals, and the entirety of pre-Islamic Arabic poetry. After migrating to Saudi Arabia following his Hajj pilgrimage in 1367 AH, he was appointed to teach at the Prophet's Mosque and founded the legal faculty at the Islamic University of Madinah.
