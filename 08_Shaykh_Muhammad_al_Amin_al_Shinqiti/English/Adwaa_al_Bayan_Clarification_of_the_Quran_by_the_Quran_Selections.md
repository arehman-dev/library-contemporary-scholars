# Clarification of the Qur'an by the Qur'an (Adwa' al-Bayan - Foundations)
### Author: Allamah Muhammad al-Amin al-Shinqiti (1325–1393 AH)
### English Translation of Methodological Excerpts & Principles

---

## The Supreme Status of Tafsir al-Qur'an bi-al-Qur'an

All praise belongs to Allah, who sent down upon His servant the Book and did not make therein any deviance.

Know, noble reader, that the noblest, most authoritative, and highest form of Qur'anic commentary (*Tafsir*) is the explanation of the Qur'an by the Qur'an itself. For no one knows the intended meaning of Allah's speech better than Allah the Almighty.

What is mentioned in generalized terms (*Mujmal*) in one passage is explained in meticulous detail (*Mufassal*) in another verse; what is stated in absolute terms (*Mutlaq*) in one surah is qualified (*Muqayyad*) elsewhere; and what appears concise in one narrative is expanded with profound legal context in another context.

---

## Methodological Rules Applied in Adwa' al-Bayan

Throughout this monumental work, several fundamental hermeneutical rules are strictly observed:

### 1. Reconciling Verses that Seemingly Differ (*Jam' Bayn al-Ayat*)
Ahl al-Sunnah firmly testify that the Qur'an contains zero internal contradiction:
> *"Do they not then reflect upon the Qur'an? If it had been from other than Allah, they would surely have found therein much contradiction."* (Surah an-Nisa 4:82)
Where superficial contradictions appear to the unlearned, authentic Arabic linguistic context, distinctions of circumstance, and levels of reality reveal perfect divine harmony.

### 2. Determining Legal Rulings Solely through Authentic Proofs
In matters of jurisprudence (*Ahkam*), we evaluate the deductions of the four classical schools (Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, Hanbali) impartially, giving precedence to whichever position is substantiated by unequivocal Qur'anic text and verified Prophetic Hadith, free from partisan fanaticism (*Ta'assub*).

### 3. Exposing the Invalidity of Man-Made Legislation (*Hukm bi Ghayr Ma Anzalallah*)
One of the most extensive themes in *Adwa' al-Bayan* is establishing the absolute authority of the Divine Law (*Shari'ah*). Replacing the clear judgements of Allah and His Messenger with secular or tribal legislation violates the core of *Tawhid al-Hakimiyyah* and constitutes grave misguidance.

## Sample Exegesis: The Opening Chapter (Surah al-Fatihah)

When Allah the Exalted says:
> **﴿اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ * صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ﴾**
> *"Guide us to the Straight Path — the path of those upon whom You have bestowed favor..."*

Who are those upon whom Allah has bestowed favor? The Qur'an itself answers this decisively in Surah an-Nisa (4:69):
> **﴿وَمَن يُطِعِ اللَّهَ وَالرَّسُولَ فَأُولَٰئِكَ مَعَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمَ اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِم مِّنَ النَّبِيِّينَ وَالصِّدِّيقِينَ وَالشُّهَدَاءِ وَالصَّالِحِينَ ۚ وَحَسُنَ أُولَٰئِكَ رَفِيقًا﴾**
> *"And whoever obeys Allah and the Messenger — those will be with the ones upon whom Allah has bestowed favor: of the prophets, the steadfast affirmers of truth, the martyrs and the righteous. And excellent are those as companions."*

Thus, the Qur'an clarifies its own terminology without leaving room for theological ambiguity.
