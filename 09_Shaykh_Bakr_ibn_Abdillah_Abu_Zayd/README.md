# Allamah Muhaqqiq Shaykh Dr. Bakr ibn Abdillah Abu Zayd
### العلامة المحقق الشيخ الدكتور بكر بن عبد الله أبو زيد
**Lifespan:** 1365–1429 AH (1946–2008 CE)  
**Scholarly Role:** President of the International Islamic Fiqh Academy, Member of Council of Senior Scholars  

---

## 📖 Biography & Scholarly Standing

Shaykh Bakr Abu Zayd was an accomplished jurist, bibliographer, and reformer who presided over the International Islamic Fiqh Academy of the OIC and served on the Permanent Committee for Ifta. Born in Shaqra, he studied under Shaykh Muhammad al-Amin al-Shinqiti and Shaykh Abd al-Aziz ibn Baz. His writings are distinguished by classical linguistic majesty and rigorous methodology, with works such as Hilyat Talib al-Ilm and Al-Madkhal al-Mufassal forming mandatory reading for serious scholars.
