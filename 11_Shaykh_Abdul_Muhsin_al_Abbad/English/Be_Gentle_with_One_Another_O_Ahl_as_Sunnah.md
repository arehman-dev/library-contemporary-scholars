# Be Gentle with One Another, O Ahl al-Sunnah!
### Author: Shaykh Allamah Abdul-Muhsin ibn Hamad al-Abbad al-Badr (b. 1353 AH)
### English Translation of the Celebrated Treatise (Rifqan Ahl al-Sunnah bi-Ahl al-Sunnah)

---

## Introduction

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.

All praise is due to Allah, Lord of all the worlds. May peace and blessings be upon His servant, Messenger, and beloved Prophet, Muhammad, and upon his family and Companions.

To proceed:
Allah the Almighty has bestowed upon the Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah the immense blessing of adhering to the Sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ and following the footsteps of the Salaf al-Salih. However, among the gravest afflictions in contemporary times is the occurrence of discord, boycotting (*Hajr*), and harsh disparagement between students of the Sunnah over secondary issues or personal grievances.

This treatise is a sincere and affectionate reminder to my brothers and sons among the Ahl al-Sunnah to adopt gentleness, mutual mercy, and wisdom in addressing disagreements.

---

## Principles of Brotherhood and Softness

### 1. The Obligation of Loving Unity
Allah the Exalted commands:
> *"And hold firmly to the rope of Allah all together and do not become divided."* (Surah Ali 'Imran 3:103)
The Prophet ﷺ said:
> *"The believers in their mutual kindness, compassion and sympathy are just like one body. When one limb suffers, the whole body responds to it with wakefulness and fever."* (Sahih al-Bukhari & Sahih Muslim)

### 2. Guarding the Tongue from Slandering Scholars
The honor of scholars and students of knowledge is sacred. Rushing to declare an upright Sunni scholar an innovator (*Tabdi'*) or expelling them from the Sunnah due to an unintentional mistake or differing legal interpretation is contrary to the methodology of the pious predecessors.

### 3. The Proper Conditions of Boycotting (*Hajr*)
Boycotting is a medicinal measure intended to reform the sinner or protect the community from manifest heresy. It must never be weaponized out of personal spite or utilized against one whose foundation is upon the Sunnah. If boycotting leads to greater corruption and enmity among the people of truth, it becomes unlawful.

### 4. Gentleness in Admonition (*Rifq*)
The Messenger of Allah ﷺ affirmed:
> *"Indeed, gentleness does not exist in anything except that it beautifies it, and it is not removed from anything except that it disgraces it."* (Sahih Muslim)

May Allah unify the hearts of the Ahl al-Sunnah, rectify their affairs, and grant them triumph upon the clear truth.
