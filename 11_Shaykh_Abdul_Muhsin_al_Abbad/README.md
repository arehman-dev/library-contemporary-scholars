# Muhaddith of Madinah Shaykh Abdul-Muhsin ibn Hamad al-Abbad
### محدث المدينة المنورة الشيخ عبد المحسن بن حمد العباد
**Lifespan:** b. 1353 AH (1934 CE – Present)  
**Scholarly Role:** Senior Traditionist, Former Vice President of Islamic University of Madinah, Teacher at the Prophet's Mosque  

---

## 📖 Biography & Scholarly Standing

Shaykh Abdul-Muhsin al-Abbad is the senior living traditionist of the Holy City of Madinah. Born in Zulfa, he completed his studies in Riyadh before moving to Madinah in 1381 AH to participate in the founding faculty of the Islamic University of Madinah, eventually serving as its Vice President. He has taught continuous public halaqat inside the Prophet's Mosque for over fifty years, completing entire explanatory cycles of the Six Canonical Hadith Books (Kutub al-Sittah).
