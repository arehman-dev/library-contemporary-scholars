## 📌 Overview & Scope

The **Library of Contemporary Scholars** (`library-contemporary-scholars`) serves as the third volume in the triple open-source Islamic scholarship corpus alongside:
1. 📖 **[Islamic Classical Library](https://github.com/arehman-dev/islamic-classical-library)** — The primary canon: Qur'an, Six Canonical Hadith, Classical Tafsir, Hadith Commentaries, Classical Textbooks, and Usool.
2. 🌟 **[Library The Golden Chain](https://github.com/arehman-dev/library-the-golden-chain)** — The complete extant legacy of the triumvirate: Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah, Imam Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyyah, and Al-Hafiz Ibn Rajab al-Hanbali.
3. 🏛️ **Library of Contemporary Scholars** (this repository) — Senior 20th and 21st-century Arab Salafi scholars who clarified, defended, and disseminated the creed of the Salaf in the modern age.

### Core Methodology & Standards
- **Option A Curation**: Core published treatises, fundamental matns, and verified reference works are directly preserved in clean `.md` Markdown format with YAML frontmatter. Direct links are provided in documentation to official multi-volume fatwa encyclopedias (e.g. 30 volumes of Ibn Baz, 29 volumes of Ibn Uthaymeen).
- **Exclusively Clean `.md` Files**: No duplicate `.txt` files or intermediate build scrapings to avoid disk bloating and maintain repository elegance.
- **Trilingual Heritage**: Preserving works across **Arabic** (original primary texts), **English** (academic & authentic translations), and **Urdu** (subcontinental scholarly translations).
- **Open Sourced & Clean Git History**: Zero automation scripts in Git tracking or history (all scrapers maintained strictly locally).
