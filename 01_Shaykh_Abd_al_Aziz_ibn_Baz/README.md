# Grand Mufti Shaykh Abd al-Aziz ibn Abdillah ibn Baz
### سماحة الشيخ عبد العزيز بن عبد الله بن باز
**Lifespan:** 1330–1420 AH (1910–1999 CE)  
**Scholarly Role:** Grand Mufti of Saudi Arabia (1413–1420 AH), Head of Council of Senior Scholars  

---

## 📖 Biography & Scholarly Standing

Shaykh Abd al-Aziz ibn Baz was one of the foremost Sunni scholars of the 20th century. Born in Riyadh, he memorized the Qur'an in his youth before losing his eyesight due to infection. He studied under leading scholars including Shaykh Muhammad ibn Ibrahim Al ash-Sheikh. He served as the Grand Mufti of Saudi Arabia, rector of the Islamic University of Madinah, and president of the Constituent Council of the Muslim World League. He was globally revered for his profound knowledge of Hadith and Fiqh, boundless generosity, and commitment to the pristine methodology of the Salaf.
