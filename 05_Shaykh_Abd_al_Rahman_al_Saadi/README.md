# Allamah Mufassir Shaykh Abd al-Rahman ibn Nasir al-Sa'di
### العلامة المفسر الشيخ عبد الرحمن بن ناصر السعدي
**Lifespan:** 1307–1376 AH (1889–1956 CE)  
**Scholarly Role:** Premier Mufassir and Teacher of Qassim, Mentor of Shaykh Ibn Uthaymeen  

---

## 📖 Biography & Scholarly Standing

Shaykh Abd al-Rahman al-Sa'di was the pioneer of contemporary Quranic exegesis and systematic pedagogical jurisprudence in Najd. Residing in Unayzah, he lost both parents in childhood yet pursued religious sciences tirelessly under scholars of Najd and the Hijaz. His magnum opus, Taysir al-Karim al-Rahman fi Tafsir Kalam al-Mannan, is renowned for its remarkable spiritual insight, linguistic eloquence, and adherence to the creed of the Salaf without scholastic obscurity.
