# Muhaddith of Yemen Shaykh Muqbil ibn Hadi al-Wadi'i
### محدث الديار اليمنية الشيخ مقبل بن هادي الوادعي
**Lifespan:** 1352–1422 AH (1933–2001 CE)  
**Scholarly Role:** Reviver of Sunnah in Yemen, Founder of Dar al-Hadith in Dammaj  

---

## 📖 Biography & Scholarly Standing

Shaykh Muqbil ibn Hadi al-Wadi'i was the revitalizer of Hadith studies and Salafi education in Yemen. Born in the Wadi'ah tribe near Sa'dah, he traveled to Saudi Arabia and studied at the Islamic University of Madinah under Shaykh al-Albani, Shaykh Ibn Baz, and Shaykh al-Shinqiti. Returning to Yemen, he established Dar al-Hadith in Dammaj, transforming it into an internationally celebrated center of classical Hadith memorization, manuscript editing, and narrator criticism.
