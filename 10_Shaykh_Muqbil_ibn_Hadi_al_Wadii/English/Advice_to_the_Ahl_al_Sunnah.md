# Sincere Advice to the Ahl al-Sunnah
### Author: Shaykh Muqbil ibn Hadi al-Wadi'i (1352–1422 AH)
### English Translation of Selected Directives

---

## Author's Introduction

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.

All praise is due to Allah, the Lord of all creation. We bear witness that there is no deity worthy of worship except Allah alone without partner, and we bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and noble Messenger.

To the Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah across the Muslim world:
May Allah preserve you upon the truth, unify your ranks upon the Sunnah, and shield you from the tribulations of sectarianism (*Hizbiyyah*), tribalism, and worldly competition.

---

## Core Counsel to the Students of Knowledge and Da'wah

### 1. Absolute Devotion to the Qur'an and Sunnah
Do not elevate the word of any contemporary individual above the clear text of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ. When an authentic hadith is verified through sound chains of transmission (*Isnad*), it is incumbent upon every believer to embrace it wholeheartedly, regardless of whether it aligns with popular societal customs.

### 2. Warning Against Partisanship and Sectarian Blocs (*Al-Hizbiyyah*)
Partisanship splits the Muslims and corrupts the heart. Loyalty (*Wala'*) and disavowal (*Bara'*) must be established strictly upon the religion of Allah, not upon allegiances to contemporary organizations, election committees, or political coalitions.

### 3. Diligence in Hadith Verification (*Takhrij*)
The preservation of the religion rests upon the sciences of Hadith and the critical evaluation of narrators (*Jarh wa Ta'dil*). A student must not narrate weak (*Da'if*) or fabricated (*Mawdu'*) traditions without clarifying their weakness.

### 4. Purification of Graves and Rejection of Shrine-Worship
The greatest evil afflicting parts of the Muslim lands is the elevation of graves, building domes over deceased scholars, and seeking intercession from the dead. We must teach the people the pristine Monotheism taught by the Prophet Ibrahim and the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ with wisdom, patience, and unwavering resolve.
