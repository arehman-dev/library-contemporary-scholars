# Shaykh Dr. Salih ibn Fawzan al-Fawzan
### معالي الشيخ الدكتور صالح بن فوزان الفوزان
**Lifespan:** b. 1354 AH (1935 CE – Present)  
**Scholarly Role:** Senior Jurist, Member of the Council of Senior Scholars & Permanent Committee (al-Lajnah al-Da'imah)  

---

## 📖 Biography & Scholarly Standing

Shaykh Salih al-Fawzan is the senior living authority of the contemporary Salafi scholarly establishment. Born in Qassim, Saudi Arabia, he completed his doctorate in comparative Islamic jurisprudence from the Higher Institute of Judiciary in Riyadh. He has served continuously on the Permanent Committee for Scholarly Research and Ifta and the Council of Senior Scholars. His books, including Al-Mulakhkhas al-Fiqhi and Sharh Kitab al-Tawhid, are standard textbooks across Islamic universities and institutes worldwide.
